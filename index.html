Privacy Policy

Last updated 5 November 2026

Who we are

Borrow My Brain provides research, project roadmapping and market intelligence services from Mumbai, India, to clients including those in Australia. This policy explains how we handle personal information collected through this website. We handle it in line with the Australian Privacy Principles where they apply.

What we collect

When you submit the enquiry form we collect your name, email address, organisation or university, whether you are a student, professional or business, the service you are interested in, your budget range, your message and your consent. We also record the time of submission. This site does not use advertising or analytics cookies.

Why we collect it

  • To respond to your enquiry, book sessions and prepare quotes.
  • To deliver the services you ask for.
  • To keep business and legal records.
  • To send follow-up messages, only where you have agreed. Every such message includes a way to unsubscribe.

Where it is stored and who sees it

Enquiries are stored in a private Google Sheet and notified by email using Google services. Google may store data on servers outside Australia and India, so your information can be handled overseas. We do not sell your information. We share it only where needed to provide the service you requested, where you agree, or where the law requires. This site also loads fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when a page loads.

Keeping it safe

We limit access to enquiry data and rely on the security features of the services above. No online system is completely secure, so please do not put sensitive personal details in your message.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry data for as long as it is needed for your enquiry, the services provided and our record-keeping obligations. After that we delete or de-identify it.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or withdraw your consent to follow-up messages, by contacting us through the enquiry form on this page.

Complaints

Contact us first and we will try to resolve it. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
